# Indian Country Deals: public preview source review

Ten selected independent events. Sources checked October 1, 2026. This is a public sample, not the complete collection or a statistical sample. Each CE identifies the Native entity associated with the deal; it does not identify its subsidiary or borrowing vehicle. Existing IDs are retained.

| Event ID | Cedar entity ID | Primary source and what it establishes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CEV-2025-01F262669F | CE-001CE-M7 | [Primary source](https://sanmanuel-nsn.gov/news/smia-completes-acquisition-waldorf-astoria-monarch-beach-resort-club). Tribal release dated October 17 announces full acquisition by SMIA affiliates, identifies the resort and golf links, and distinguishes earlier partial ownership. Exact legal closing date and price are not disclosed. |
| CEV-2025-09E060E63E | CE-00143-AM | [Primary source](https://investor.citizensbank.com/about-us/newsroom/latest-news/2025/2025-12-11.aspx). Lender release dated December 11 states $390 million debt financing for the Dry Creek Rancheria gaming authority, led by Citizens and anchored by GLPI. This is financing capacity, not a disbursement or casino opening. |
| CEV-2025-101F917A66 | CE-0018H-JJ | [Primary source](https://pcifs.com/news/suprtek-aquisition-announcement). PCI Federal release dated April 1 identifies the acquired company and expressly states that PCI Federal is owned by Poarch Creek Indians. No price or exact closing date is disclosed. |
| CEV-2025-1F1A049EDC | CE-001A9-CA | [Primary source](https://casino.hardrock.com/brighton/newsroom/2025/02/seminole-brighton-bay-hotel-casino-stages-grand-opening-and-celebration). Casino operator release dated February 6 reports the actual grand opening that day, identifies Seminole tribal ownership and the Brighton Reservation, and states that the new resort replaces Casino Brighton. |
| CEV-2025-249EC2AE05 | CE-0015A-SK | [Primary source](https://www.clm.com/steve-mcsloy-represents-banco-santander-for-tribal-and-federal-indian-law-advice-in-connection-with-a-200-million-term-loan-b-extended-to-the-route-66-development-authority/). Counsel announcement dated January 30, 2025 establishes a $200 million term loan B extended to Route 66 Development Authority, an arm of the Iowa Tribe of Oklahoma. It does not separately establish closing or cash-disbursement date. |
| CEV-2025-4D8A6B38A7 | CE-001AF-G0 | [Primary source](https://snoqualmietribe.us/snoqualmie-casino-hotel-grand-opening/). Tribal August 22, 2025 report explicitly dates the hotel grand opening to August 8, 2025. Remove unneeded original-casino opening-year claim from this event. |
| CEV-2025-6CAA2D9284 | CE-0007G-30 | [Primary source](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-finalizes-major-alaskan-land-transfer-nana-regional-corporation). Interior July 16, 2025 release announces completed conveyance of nearly 28,000 acres under ANCSA Section 12(c). July 16 is the announcement date; the instrument execution date is not separately stated. |
| CEV-2025-77402471C6 | CE-001CK-J4 | [Primary source](https://www.yuroktribe.org/post/largest-ever-land-back-conservation-deal-in-california-now-complete). Yurok release has June 5, 2025 dateline and June 9 website posting. It explicitly states final 14,968-acre transfer closed May 30. The 47,097-acre whole effort and $56 million whole-family acquisition cost must not be assigned as this final-parcel purchase price. |
| CEV-2021-002D0E2F52 | CE-0017R-QH | [Primary source](https://waseyabek.com/waseyabek-development-company-invests-in-bamf-health/). November 16, 2021 buyer release confirms NHBP ownership of WDC, $3 million passive BAMF Series B investment; $30 million is the whole raise, not WDC investment. Announcement supports completed-by-release status, not exact settlement date. |
| CEV-2021-357C5BC869 | CE-0018K-Y4 | [Primary source](https://www.mno-bmadsen.com/news/mno-acquires-walerko). Buyer March 18 release identifies Mno-Bmadsen as Pokagon investment arm, Mno-DREK vehicle and Walerko target. It expressly says closed in March and terms undisclosed. |

Source categories represented here are tribal government releases, tribal enterprise or operator announcements, lender announcements, transaction-counsel announcements, and government land-transfer releases. These ten publisher domains are ten resources; they do not establish any new total across all Cedar datasets.

Only three sample events carry money values: River Rock financing of $390 million, Route 66 loan principal of $200 million, and Waséyabek's $3 million equity investment. These values describe different instruments and are not presented as a combined total. Yurok's $56 million project-wide value is intentionally excluded from the final parcel event.

The consumer schema remains the existing 40-column public-preview schema. Exact announcement, closing-month and opening semantics are retained in Event_Type and Notes. The full governed export uses the separately declared modern schema.
