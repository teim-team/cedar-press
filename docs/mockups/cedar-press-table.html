What changes

  1. The dropdown becomes the rail. Picking a collection is a <select> today whose own label is clipped behind its chevron (“All 12 open collections (search acr…”). The door picks the same twelve with twelve marks, which is the thing this page was built to show.
  2. Eight columns become five. The arrow column goes (the row opens the record), entity type joins the identifier line, and Source becomes the arrow at the end of the record it belongs to.
  3. Nothing is clipped mid-word. Today the entity name, the alias line, the observation and the amount’s basis all end in an ellipsis or run off the right edge — four truncations a row.
  4. The Cedar id is teal and always the second line, the way the door sets it. It is the one field on the row no other product has, and today it is grey and indistinguishable from the alias beneath it.
  5. The toolbar becomes a head and a caption band. Controls sit in the pane head; what is in view and how much of it moves into the mono band the door already uses.
  6. On a phone the rail is a strip of marks across the top and the rows stack, which is what the door does at 390px. Today this table scrolls sideways.

Rows: first twelve of “All 12 open collections”, read from the running app 2026-09-20. Marks: src/pages/grove/pressCollectionIcons.jsx. Row counts and shelves: data/cedar/collections.manifest.json. The proposed side uses the real .cp-app__* and .cp-pane__* rules from src/styles/grove/press.css; the “now” side the real .cp-ex__* ones. Both ship in this repository today.
