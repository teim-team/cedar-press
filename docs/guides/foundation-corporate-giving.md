### Units and formats

- Money columns are nominal US dollars as recorded, no rounding: `amount_exact_usd`, `amount_lower_usd`, `amount_upper_usd`, `amount_aggregate_usd`. The `currency` column states the reported currency of each row where it is filled; a blank currency is a gap, not an assumption of US dollars.
- Dates are ISO 8601 calendar dates (YYYY-MM-DD). Where a precision column (`term_start_precision`, `term_end_precision`) states `month` or `year`, the date is written to that precision (YYYY-MM or YYYY) and no day is invented.
- Identifiers, codes, FIPS and EIN values are text and keep their leading zeros.
