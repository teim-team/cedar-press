from chambers (advocacy) and CDFIs (finance). 2 rows in

(Yesterday's re-check round was empty; this frame was tried before declaring a

  Hopi Nations; 76 businesses funded, cohorts named in announcements. A

## Discipline

Incubator/TA participation is opt-in and program-defined — never an ownership
or citizenship certification — and cohort members are person/business records
(person boundary). Change Labs is Discovery Only; ONABEN is a partnership lead
with no enumerable list. No registry rows added.
