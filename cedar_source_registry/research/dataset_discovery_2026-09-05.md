# Dataset discovery — 2026-09-05 round: tribal business registries

Frame: nations' (and states') own business-entity registries — the
enumeration lead flagged 2026-09-04 from the CTA's "Tribal jurisdiction
secretary of state" language. 3 rows in
`dataset_discovery_2026-09-05.jsonl` (WebSearch-only; URLs literal).

## The find that stands out

**Utah's Division of Corporations has a dedicated "Tribal Business Entity"
registration type** — an entity created under Tribal Law files under a Tribal
option and is searchable in Utah's free public Business Entity Search by
name, principal, or registered agent. A state SOS applying a specific
tribal-law category is rare and valuable: it enumerates tribal-law entities
operating in Utah and validates their standing and principals. It refines the
2026-08-27 "Utah state certification landscape" placeholder into a concrete
artifact, and raises the question of which other states expose a comparable
category.

## The frame-map and the prototype

- **BIA/IEED Corporation Codes** — a coverage-frame meta-source: which
  nations have enacted corporate codes, and thus can run their own registries.
  Enumerates no businesses; it targets the hunt.
- **Navajo Nation Corporation Filing Office** — the tribal SOS-equivalent
  holding domestic/foreign incorporations on Navajo. Honest partial: no public
  online search surfaced (physical filing office; a fee-schedule PDF exists).
  Explicitly bounded against the Navajo NBOA Source List already in the
  registry (TBD-041) — the NBOA roster is a certified Navajo-owned priority
  list; this incorporation registry is any corp on Navajo, identity-mixed.

## Discipline

Registration under a tribal-entity category, or incorporation on a
reservation, is a tribal-nexus signal, not proof of ownership share or member
citizenship — do_not_infer. The Navajo row is bounded against TBD-041 to
avoid re-proposing a known source. No registry rows added.
