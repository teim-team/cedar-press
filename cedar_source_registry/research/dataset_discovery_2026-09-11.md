# Dataset discovery — 2026-09-11 round: Native women-owned business networks

Frame: networks and directories of Native women-owned businesses. 2 rows in
`dataset_discovery_2026-09-11.jsonl` (WebSearch-only; URLs literal).

## The find

- **Native Women Lead — 2022 Business Resource Directory** — a downloadable
  PDF (hosted on the NM Indian Affairs Dept site) listing 100+ Native
  women-owned businesses across 30+ Tribal Nations, by industry. One of the
  few genuinely enumerable individual-Native-business directories surfaced in
  any round — the women-owned slice of the hard-to-find individual layer.
- **ONAC Resource Guide for Native Women Business Owners (2021)** — a resource
  guide (support/capital/TA), not a roster; recorded as the companion.

## Discipline

The NWL directory is self-identified, org-curated, NM-centered, and a fixed
2022 snapshot; entries are person/business records (owner names) so the person
boundary applies pre-FIELD_CLASSIFICATION. "Women-owned + Native self-ID" is
the inclusion basis, not a tribal-citizenship certification. Cross-Reference /
Discovery grade. No registry rows added.
