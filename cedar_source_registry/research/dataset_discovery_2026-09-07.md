# Dataset discovery — 2026-09-07 round: Native CDFIs

Frame: Native Community Development Financial Institutions — the financing
layer that sits between capital and Native businesses. 3 rows in
`dataset_discovery_2026-09-07.jsonl` (WebSearch-only; URLs literal).

## The layer, and how it feeds the dataset

- **CDFI Fund Certified CDFI List (Native subset)** — the authoritative roster
  of Native CDFIs (69 as of 2025-03-12), downloadable, with the Fed
  Minneapolis map as a companion. This is the lender universe.
- **Native CDFI Network** — the membership-org directory of the same
  institutions, plus profiles.
- **Four Bands Community Fund** — one worked example of the payoff: a Native
  CDFI whose site names client businesses in success stories and incubator
  spotlights. Every institution on the CDFI Fund list potentially offers the
  same borrower-showcase route to individual Native businesses.

## Why it matters

The CDFI list is a hub: it enumerates the ~69 lenders, and each lender is a
discovery route to the individual borrower businesses the registry is hardest
to find. It also ties into the financing story behind the deals dataset and
the enterprise ties in NEST.

## Discipline (do_not_infer)

- A **Native CDFI is an institution, not a Native-owned business**, and the
  ">=50% of activities serving Native communities" test is an activity test,
  not an ownership certification. The first two rows are Coverage Frame.
- **Borrower spotlights are Discovery Only**: opt-in marketing, partial, and
  person/business records (person boundary). Being a CDFI borrower is not an
  ownership or citizenship assertion. No registry rows added.
