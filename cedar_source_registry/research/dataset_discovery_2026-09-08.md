# Dataset discovery — 2026-09-08 round: Native-owned banks (depository layer)

Frame: Native-owned depository institutions — the bank layer, distinct from
yesterday's CDFIs (loan funds, activity-defined). 2 rows in
`dataset_discovery_2026-09-08.jsonl` (WebSearch-only; URLs literal).

## The two regulators, both authoritative

- **FDIC MDI List (Native American subset)** — 20 Native American / Alaskan
  Native American MDIs of 148 total (2023-12-31), downloadable Excel/PDF,
  quarterly + historical to 2001, with the Minority Banking Opportunity
  Explorer map. The FDIC-supervised side.
- **OCC Native American Banking resource directory** — the national-bank side,
  including the "Guide to Tribal Ownership of a National Bank."

Flagship: Native American Bank, N.A. (2001) — multi-tribally owned, 29 of 35
shareholders are Tribes, Tribal Enterprises or ANCs.

## Why it matters

A tribally-owned bank is also a tribal **enterprise**, so this layer
crosswalks to the holding-company / NEST work. And it completes the Native
finance picture begun with CDFIs: loan funds (CDFIs) + depositories (MDI
banks).

## Discipline (do_not_infer)

The MDI definition has two prongs: (a) >=51% minority-owned voting stock, or
(b) majority-minority board + predominantly-minority community served. **Prong
(b) is not an ownership fact** — so MDI status corroborates Native ownership
only under prong (a); record which prong. Both rows are Coverage Frame
(institutions and reference material), not business rosters. No registry rows
added.
